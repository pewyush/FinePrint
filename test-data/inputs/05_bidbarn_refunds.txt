BidBarn Marketplace — Refund and Cancellation Policy (saved excerpt, test input 5)

You may cancel an order before it ships and receive a full refund. Once an order has shipped, refunds are only available for items that arrive damaged, and you must report damage within 48 hours of delivery.

For digital items, all sales are final and no refunds are given once the download link has been opened.

Sellers set their own return windows between 7 and 30 days. BidBarn is not a party to the sale contract between you and the seller.

If we cancel your listing we may withhold pending payouts for up to 90 days to cover chargebacks and disputes.

Monthly subscription fees for BidBarn Seller Pro renew automatically until cancelled. Cancellation must be done at least 3 days before the renewal date.

We may change these refund rules at any time by posting the updated version on this page.

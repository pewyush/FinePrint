ShopEasy — Privacy Policy (saved excerpt, test input 2)

We collect your name, email address, device identifiers and payment information when you create an account or complete a purchase.

We share your data with payment processors, delivery partners and advertising vendors. We do not sell your data for money, but we do share it with advertising partners for targeted ads.

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. If your account is inactive for 24 months we may delete it.

You may request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by emailing privacy@shopeasy.example. We respond within 30 days.

If you are under 16 you must not create an account on ShopEasy.

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your cart and to measure how our site is used. You can disable cookies in your browser, but the cart will not work.

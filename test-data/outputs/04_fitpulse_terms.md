FitPulse is a fitness app that gathers your workout, heart rate, sleep, and medical activity data and can share it in bulk with other companies. It forces you to settle any disputes through binding arbitration and you can’t join a class action. If you cancel a yearly plan, you’ll lose 25% of the remaining months as a fee, and the company can change the rules anytime, which you accept by continuing to use the app. You can delete your personal account, but the overall statistics made from your data will stay.
